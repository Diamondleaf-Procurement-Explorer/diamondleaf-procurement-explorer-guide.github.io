Diamondleaf Procurement Explorer

What this explorer shows

The Diamondleaf Public Procurement Explorer provides structured visibility into public procurement activity across multiple domains, including EU tenders and related datasets. It is designed to help users explore patterns, identify opportunities, and understand procurement dynamics without needing to work directly with raw source data.

The focus is on making complex procurement data easier to navigate and interpret, while maintaining a clear link back to the original published information.

Data domains and reference layers

The Diamondleaf Public Procurement Explorer is built on multiple source-aligned schemas that combine procurement data, supporting reference data, and legal or statistical context. Together, these layers make it possible to move from raw published notices to more structured interpretation, while preserving traceability to the original source systems.

Some schemas contain operational procurement data, such as notices and related market information. Others provide the supporting structure needed to interpret that data correctly, including code lists, business term definitions, API field references, legal field mappings, classification systems, and statistical indicators. This separation helps keep the platform transparent: users can work with higher-level analytics while still understanding the underlying field logic and source context.

  • DIAMONDLEAF_PLATFORM_DATA_QUALITY_AND_SYSTEM_STATE
    Provides operational context about the platform itself, including data availability, completeness, and system status indicators. It helps users understand how current and reliable the underlying datasets are and whether any data-quality limitations may affect interpretation.
  • ENTERPRISE_EUROPE_NETWORK
    Contains company profiles, partnership opportunities, and related market signals. This layer complements procurement data by adding broader visibility into organizations and cross-border business activity.
  • TENDERS_ELECTRONIC_DAILY
    Represents EU public procurement notices, including procedures, contract awards, and related metadata. This is the core procurement dataset within the Explorer.
  • NORTH_ATLANTIC_TREATY_ORGANIZATION
    Includes procurement-related data connected to NATO activity, extending coverage beyond EU civilian procurement into international contracting environments.
  • EU_NOTICES_CODELIST_REFERENCE_DATA
    Contains standardized code lists such as procedure types and classification values, supporting consistent filtering and interpretation across datasets.
  • EU_NOTICES_BUSINESS_TERM_FIELD_LIST_REFERENCE_DATA
    Provides official business terms from the eForms SDK, linking procurement concepts to their technical field representation.
  • EU_NOTICES_API_FIELD_REFERENCE_DATA
    Documents source API fields, including naming and structure, supporting transparency and traceability to original data sources.
  • REGULATORY_AND_LEGAL_FIELD_REFERENCE_DATA
    Connects data fields to legal and regulatory definitions, supporting compliance and interpretation in a governance context.
  • STATISTICAL_CLASSIFICATION_NOMENCLATURES
    Includes classification systems such as CPV, NACE, and NUTS, enabling consistent categorization across sectors and regions.
  • STATISTICAL_INDICATORS
    Contains derived metrics and indicators that summarize patterns and trends, supporting higher-level analysis without requiring manual calculations.

How to read the analytics

The analytics are designed to highlight patterns and relationships rather than provide definitive answers. Users are encouraged to interpret results in context, taking into account data completeness, classification differences, and potential gaps in reporting.

Where possible, metrics are derived consistently across datasets, but differences in source structures and publication practices may still influence outcomes.

Navigation

Use filters and selection options to refine the dataset according to your needs. Most views are interactive and allow for drilling down into more detailed levels of information.

Limitations

The platform reflects publicly available data and is subject to the limitations of those sources. Not all procurement activity is fully captured or consistently reported across regions or time periods.

Users should treat the outputs as indicative rather than exhaustive and consider additional validation where necessary.
